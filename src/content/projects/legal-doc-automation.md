A document automation platform for a law firm that replaced their manual paralegal drafting workflow with a self-serve flow. Clients fill in a structured form; the system maps submissions onto a library of legal document templates and produces a ready-to-review document at submission time — saving **hours per case** in attorney time.

Delivered as a 3-month engagement. Python handled the templating engine and document assembly; a Node.js service fronted the form intake, validation, and submission lifecycle.
