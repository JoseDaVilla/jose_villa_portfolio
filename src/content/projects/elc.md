A real-time call exchange that powers a leads marketplace. Sellers list inbound call inventory; buyers configure their criteria; the routing layer matches and connects calls **live** as they come in.

Behind the matching engine sits the operational machinery a marketplace actually needs to run: payouts, dispute handling, reporting, and the data flow needed to settle who-bought-what at the end of each cycle. Two-sided marketplaces only work when both sides trust the math.

Built on Node.js with PostgreSQL, designed for sub-second routing latency under sustained call volume.
